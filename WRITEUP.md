Sentence embedding runs an algorithm to create vector representations of the roles terms play in a sentence. Columns in embeddings are meaningless explicitly, but can occasionally appear to contain relationships between terms as generated by the embedding, sometimes making them able to extrapolate nuanced relationships between terms.
